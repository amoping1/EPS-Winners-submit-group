How a forecast is produced

Two of the six steps are agents that decide their own next move. The other four are fixed code, deliberately: the last word on a number belongs to something that cannot be argued round.

Agents (decide for themselves)

  • Research agent — writes its own queries, reads, re-queries when evidence is thin
  • Three forecasters — guidance, statistical, qualitative; separate contexts
  • Critic — sees the numbers but not the reasoning, tries to refute

Fixed code (cannot be argued round)

  • Retrieval — BM25 with a hard as_of date cutoff
  • Aggregator — sorts evidence into channels, flags thin metrics
  • Reconcile — confidence weighting, outliers cut
  • Rails — units, basis, period, guidance bound, history clamp

Why consensus matters

  • Score is our error ÷ Wall Street’s error
  • Consensus is therefore the benchmark, not a hint
  • Matching it scores ~1.0; beating it needs a reason
  • Hays is the one place we deviate deliberately
